# Add your images here

Place your project screenshots and images in this folder and subfolders:

- `projects/khussa-master/` — Screenshots from Khussa Master (Shopify dashboard, product listings, social media posts)
- `projects/daraz/` — Screenshots from your Daraz store
- `projects/alfa-mall/` — Screenshots from your Alfa Mall store
- `designs/` — Canva designs, AI product images, graphic design samples
- `products/` — Product photos

## Usage

Reference images in pages using: `/images/projects/khussa-master/image.jpg`

Supported formats: JPG, PNG, WebP, SVG
